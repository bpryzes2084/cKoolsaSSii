Privacy Policy

Last updated October 3, 2026

  • We collect only what we need to take, ship and support your order.
  • Payments are handled by Square. We never see or store your full card number.
  • We don't sell your personal information, and the site has no advertising trackers.

This policy explains how cKool n saSSii, a division of BSM Enterpryzes ("we", "us"), collects and uses information when you visit ckoolsassii.biz or buy from us.

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, the items you buy and what you paid. You enter these on Square's secure checkout page.
  • Payment details: your card is processed by Square. We receive confirmation of payment and limited details such as the card brand and last four digits, never the full card number.
  • Shipping quote details: the ZIP code you enter in your cart to see UPS shipping rates.
  • Messages: anything you send us when you contact us.
  • Technical information: our hosting and security providers automatically record basic information such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, to keep the site running and protect it from abuse.

Your cart

Your cart is saved in your own browser (local storage) so it's still there when you come back. It stays on your device and isn't sent to us until you check out. Clearing your browser data removes it.

How we use it

  • To process payment, pack and ship your order, and send order and shipping updates with tracking.
  • To answer questions and handle returns, exchanges and refunds.
  • To keep our records for accounting, tax and legal purposes.
  • To keep the site secure and working.

We don't send marketing emails unless you've agreed to receive them, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the companies that help us run the store, and only what they need:

  • Square: payment processing, checkout, receipts and order records.
  • UPS: shipping rates, labels and delivery.
  • Cloudflare and GitHub: website hosting, security and delivery.
  • Our email provider: sending order and shipping emails.

We may also share information when the law requires it, or to protect our rights or prevent fraud. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as we need them for accounting, tax and legal requirements, typically up to seven years. Information we no longer need is deleted.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to records we must keep by law. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state's privacy law. Contact us at and we'll respond within 30 days.

Security

The site uses encrypted connections (HTTPS), and payments are handled entirely on Square's secure checkout. No method of sending or storing data is perfectly secure, but we limit who can access your information to what's needed to fulfill your order.

Children

Our store is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

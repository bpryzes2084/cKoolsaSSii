Store policies

Shipping

Last updated October 3, 2026

  • We ship with UPS Ground within the United States.
  • Orders usually ship within 1–3 business days.
  • You'll get an email with your UPS tracking number when your order ships.

Where we ship

We currently ship to addresses in the United States. UPS doesn't deliver to P.O. boxes, so please use a street address. We don't ship internationally at this time.

Shipping cost

Shipping is calculated by UPS from your ZIP code and the weight of your order. Enter your ZIP code in your cart to see the exact UPS Ground price before you check out. The shipping charge is added to your total on the payment page.

When your order ships

Orders usually leave us within 1–3 business days of payment (Monday to Friday, excluding holidays). During busy periods and new releases it can take a little longer, and we'll email you if there's a delay.

Delivery time

Once shipped, UPS Ground typically delivers in 1–5 business days depending on distance. Delivery dates are estimates from UPS and aren't guaranteed.

Tracking

When your label is created we email you a tracking number with a link to follow your package on ups.com.

Address accuracy

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us right away at . We can update the address only before the order ships. Packages returned to us because of an incorrect address can be re-shipped at your cost.

Lost, delayed or stolen packages

If your tracking hasn't updated for several days, or it shows delivered but you can't find your package, check with neighbors and around your building first, then contact us within 7 days of the expected delivery date. We'll open a claim with UPS and work with you on a replacement or refund once the claim is resolved.

Damaged in transit

If your order arrives damaged, keep the packaging and email us a photo within 7 days. See our Returns policy for how we make it right.
